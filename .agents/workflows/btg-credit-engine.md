### AI Journey Capture

Before closing the phase, determine whether AI materially influenced an
engineering decision.

Record an entry only when the interaction includes meaningful reasoning,
correction, trade-off, failed assumption, or independent finding.

If relevant:
- update `ai-journey/prompts.md`
- update `ai-journey/learnings.md` only when a reusable lesson exists

If not relevant:
report `NO_JOURNEY_ENTRY`.

Do not manufacture an interaction merely to populate the AI Journey.
